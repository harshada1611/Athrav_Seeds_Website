Our Journey

Rooted in Excellence

Founded in 1998, Athrav Agro Seeds began with a vision to empower farmers with premium onion seeds. From a small family-run operation, we’ve grown into a trusted name across India, delivering high-yield, disease-resistant seeds.

Our journey is built on innovation, trust, and a deep connection with the farming community. We continuously evolve to meet the challenges of modern agriculture, ensuring sustainable growth for farmers and the environment.

Growing Together, Since 1998
1998

Founded Athrav Agro Seeds with a focus on quality onion seeds.

2005

Launched our first hybrid onion seed variety.

2012

Expanded operations to serve farmers across multiple states.

2020

Introduced disease-resistant seeds from our research farm.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To lead the agricultural industry by providing high-quality seeds that drive sustainable farming and ensure food security for future generations.

Our Mission

To innovate and deliver superior onion seeds through research, ensuring high germination, healthy crops, and farmer prosperity while protecting the environment.
